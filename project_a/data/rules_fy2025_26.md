# Statutory Income Tax Rules — FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27)

Governed by the **Finance Act, 2025** (Budget 2025 proposals).
Scope: **Salaried Individuals in India** (Section 15 to 17).

---

## 1. Statutory Deductions from Salary

### Section 16(ia) — Standard Deduction
- **Old Regime:** Retained at **₹50,000** or actual gross salary.
- **New Regime (§ 115BAC):** Retained at **₹75,000** (or actual gross salary).

---

## 2. Updated New Tax Regime Slabs (§ 115BAC) — FY 2025-26

The slab structure under Section 115BAC was widened by Finance Act, 2025 to provide relief:

| Net Taxable Income Slab | Income Tax Rate | Cumulative Base Tax |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Up to ₹4,00,000 | **Nil** | ₹0 |
| ₹4,00,001 to ₹8,00,000 | **5%** of (Income - ₹4,00,000) | ₹20,000 |
| ₹8,00,001 to ₹12,00,000 | ₹20,000 + **10%** of (Income - ₹8,00,000) | ₹60,000 |
| ₹12,00,001 to ₹16,00,000 | ₹60,000 + **15%** of (Income - ₹12,00,000) | ₹1,20,000 |
| ₹16,00,001 to ₹20,00,000 | ₹1,20,000 + **20%** of (Income - ₹16,00,000) | ₹2,00,000 |
| ₹20,00,001 to ₹24,00,000 | ₹2,00,000 + **25%** of (Income - ₹20,00,000) | ₹3,00,000 |
| Above ₹24,00,000 | ₹3,00,000 + **30%** of (Income - ₹24,00,000) | — |

---

## 3. Section 87A Full Rebate Enhancement (New Regime)

- **Eligibility:** Resident Individual whose Net Taxable Income does not exceed **₹12,00,000**.
- **Full Rebate Amount:** 100% of income tax payable or **₹60,000**, whichever is less.
- **Effective Zero-Tax Threshold:** Salaried individuals earning up to **₹12,75,000** pay **₹0 tax** under the New Regime (₹12,75,000 salary - ₹75,000 standard deduction = ₹12,00,000 taxable $\to$ 87A rebate wipes out full ₹60,000 tax).

---

## 4. Old Regime Status
- Old Regime slabs and ₹5,00,000 Section 87A limit remain **completely unchanged** (deliberately discouraged by the Ministry of Finance).
